Enjoy Tours

Constantine is a marketplace of audio tours — built around real places by people who know them. Here's what to expect as a listener.

Browse stories curated by locals and experts

Find tours by city, topic, language, or favourite creator. Every tour is built around a real place — a museum hall, a neighbourhood, a hike, a cathedral — and comes with audio, photos, and the kind of context only an insider can give.

Buy once, listen anytime

A tour is yours after a single purchase — no subscription, no expiry. Open it from your library whenever you visit the place, pause when something catches your eye, and pick up where you left off.

Navigate with confidence

Each stop has a precise location. A built-in compass points you toward the next stop, a map shows how the venues connect, and walking directions tell you what to expect on the way. Walk, listen, and explore at your own pace.

Be a Tour Creator

If you know a place better than anyone, Constantine gives you the tools to turn that into a polished audio tour — and to earn from it.

Tell the stories you know best

Whether it's the alleys of your hometown, the collection of a small museum, or a hike only you've walked, you start with what you already know. The studio walks you through structuring it into a tour, step by step.

Build it your way

Group stops into venues — rooms, squares, viewpoints — upload audio clips, attach photos, write transitions, and pin every stop on a real map. Preview each step as you go, and edit any time after publishing.

Reach a global audience

Translators on the platform can offer localised versions of your tour, expanding your reach without extra work. You set the price; payments are handled via Stripe and paid out to your account directly.
