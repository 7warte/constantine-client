Our mission

To put the world's stories
in your headphones.

Constantine connects curious travellers with the people who can really tell a place — its history, its quirks, the corners you'd never find in a guidebook. We're a marketplace where anyone with deep local knowledge can build an audio tour, and anyone visiting can hear the place as a local would.

Explorer

Stories curated by locals and experts

Tours are organised by city, topic, language, or creator. Whether you want a brisk hour through central Rome or a deep dive into a single museum hall, you'll find someone who's already done the homework. Every tour starts as a real place, told by someone who knows it well.

Buy once, listen anytime

A tour is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no expiry. Open it from your library the moment you arrive, pause when something catches your eye, and pick up where you left off, even months later. Tours you've bought stay yours.

Walk at your own pace

Every stop is pinned on a real map. A built-in compass points you toward the next stop, walking directions tell you what to expect on the way, and you decide when to move on. No tour guide checking their watch, no group to keep up with.

Hear it in your language

Tours can be translated into multiple languages by trusted contributors on the platform. The original creator stays at the centre — translations are reviewed and tied to the same tour, so you experience the same story in the voice you're most at home with.

Creator

Tell the stories you know best

Whether it's the alleys of your hometown, the collection of a small museum, or a hike only you've walked, you start with what you already know. The studio walks you through structuring it into a tour — venues, stops, audio, and the connective tissue in between.

Build it your way in the studio

Group stops into venues — rooms, squares, viewpoints — upload audio clips, attach photos, write transitions, and pin every stop on a real map. Preview every step as you go, and edit any time after publishing. Your tour, your pace.

Reach a global audience

Translators on the platform offer localised versions of your tour, expanding your reach without extra work. You set the price, you stay attributed as the original creator, and every translated listener still arrives via your tour.

Get paid for what you know

Payments are handled through Stripe and paid out directly to your account. You see every sale, every review, every payout in your studio dashboard. No middlemen, no hidden cuts — what your listeners pay is what you earn (minus standard processing fees).
